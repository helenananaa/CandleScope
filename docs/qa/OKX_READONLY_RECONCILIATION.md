# OKX read-only reconciliation

The account panel's Reconcile tab checks retained exchange data on demand through the protected desktop session. It does not place orders or change settings. Monetary fields remain decimal strings and results are separated by settlement currency.

- Fill bill IDs, instrument/order/trade references, fee currency, fee, realized P&L and trading cash change are compared with account bills. Isolated collateral changes are included in the cash identity, not treated as trading expenses. Unpaired records remain unresolved.
- Cash continuity is checked per currency using each bill's balance and signed change. The opening balance is **derived from the earliest retained bill**, not independently proved. Missing bills are unknown, not zero. The last bill is compared with the separately timed cash snapshot; differences above the displayed 1e-12 currency-unit rounding allowance require a fresh read and investigation. All differences remain visible.
- Historical positions are obtained from the read-only SWAP positions-history endpoint. Pagination uses `uTime` and retains all records sharing a page boundary. Failed, stalled, over-budget or stale reads cannot become complete evidence. A failed optional history read preserves core account records and the last good history, while marking the evidence unavailable.
- When fills-history omits margin mode, an exactly linked trade bill supplies it. Ordinary fully closed positions require query coverage, an opening and closing boundary, matching contract quantities, weighted remaining entry basis, source fee/P&L totals and the realized-net identity. The inverse calculation uses reciprocal prices. Rounding allowance is displayed: 1e-8 settlement units per fill for linear contracts, 1e-12 for inverse contracts. Actual differences remain visible.
- A funding bill is attributed only when instrument, margin mode, currency, interval and contract exposure identify a unique position, no fill occurs within five seconds of settlement, and the allocated total equals the position's reported funding total. Overlapping histories, reused cumulative snapshots, missing opposite-leg history, and same-size hedge ambiguity remain unresolved. Attribution is derived evidence, not an exchange-provided side on the bill. Original facts are unchanged.

The report is bounded to 20,000 persisted facts and 500 displayed details per section, with explicit truncation. Position history is bounded to 20 pages / 5,000 records and refreshed no more frequently than once per minute. Completed queries do not prove exchange retention of every historical event. Liquidation/ADL and unsupported position adjustments remain pending separate reconciliation. A synthetic inverse test is not live inverse-account acceptance.

Primary field definitions: [OKX API v5, account positions history and bills](https://app.okx.com/docs-v5/en/).
